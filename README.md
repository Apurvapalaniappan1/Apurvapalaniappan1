# Apurva Palaniappan

### Robotics & Autonomous Systems | Robot Perception | Autonomous Navigation

I'm a Master's student in **Robotics and Autonomous Systems at the University of Turku, Finland**, with a Bachelor's degree in Robotics and Automation.

My academic projects span multi-robot navigation, embedded control, multi-agent systems, and deep learning. I'm interested in how robots combine perception, decision-making, and control to operate autonomously.

## Selected Projects

### [Cooperative Multi-Robot Navigation](https://github.com/Apurvapalaniappan1/cooperative-multi-robot-navigation)

**Python | ROS 2 | Gazebo | TurtleBot3**

Implemented a three-robot navigation system in simulation, integrating triangle formation control, collision avoidance, connectivity maintenance, and waypoint navigation.

### [Autonomous Underwater Camera Control System](https://github.com/Apurvapalaniappan1/autonomous-underwater-camera-control)

**Python | Raspberry Pi | GNSS | Embedded Control**

Contributed to a team capstone project for shallow-water vegetation mapping, implementing camera-depth control using GNSS position, bathymetry data, and a motorized winch. The system underwent laboratory and partial field testing.

### [Deep Learning for Visual Perception](https://github.com/Apurvapalaniappan1/deep-learning-visual-perception)

**PyTorch | Computer Vision | Object Detection | RGB-D Segmentation**

Developed and evaluated Faster R-CNN and RetinaNet models for car detection, alongside a dual-stream RGB-D semantic segmentation model combining colour and depth information.

### [Autonomous Hospital Multi-Agent System](https://github.com/Apurvapalaniappan1/autonomous_hospital_multi_agent_system)

**Python | SPADE-BDI | Multi-Agent Systems**

Implemented nurse and cleaning agents for a simulated hospital environment, combining medicine delivery, emergency response, and cleaning with priority-based decisions and BFS navigation.

### [CNN-Based Image Classification and Hyperparameter Analysis](https://github.com/Apurvapalaniappan1/cnn-hyperparameter-analysis-cifar100)

**TensorFlow | Keras | CNNs | CIFAR-100**

Built CNN classifiers from scratch for two balanced 10-class CIFAR-100 subsets. Compared architecture and training settings across 36 training runs per complete execution, analysing performance differences and experimental limitations.
